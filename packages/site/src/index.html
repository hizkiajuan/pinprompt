The prompt

Short on purpose.

Your AI tool needs to find the code and know what to do, not read a DOM dump. Each pin becomes two lines: what the element is, in names it can search for, and what should change.

~250characters a pin 10pins a tab, across routes

How it works

Three moves, and you never leave the page.

1
Ctrl Shift P⇧ ⌘ P on a Mac

Start commenting

From anywhere, or press C on the page. Hover names the React component; clicks never reach your app.

2
Click Drag

Pin it and say what to change

A numbered pin drops on the element and a comment box opens right there. Ctrl Enter saves.

3
Copy prompt

Paste into your AI tool

One click on the bubble copies every pin as one prompt. Pins stay put for the next round, even through a reload.

What it knows

A

Your components, by name

Reads React's dev build for the component and its parents, so the prompt says PlanCard > BillingPage, not "the third div".

B

Fluent in shadcn/ui

Spots Button, Card, Input and every other part from data-slot, on Tailwind v3 and v4.

C

Out of your app's way

Its UI sits in a shadow root: your styles can't break it, it can't break yours. Drag the bubble to any corner.

D

Nothing leaves your browser

No account, no server, no analytics. It runs on localhost, or on a LAN or tailnet host once you allow it.

Install Pinprompt.

Free, for Chrome 130 and later. Open your dev server, press Ctrl Shift P, start pinning.

Install by hand

  1. 01Download __ZIP_NAME__ and unzip it.
  2. 02Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. 03Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. 04Pin Pinprompt to the toolbar. localhost works right away.

About

Open source, built in the open.

Pinprompt started as a tool Juan needed: point at a broken bit of a React app and hand an AI tool one exact request instead of a paragraph of "the second card, no, the other one".

Juan builds small tools for everyday work and publishes them at hizkiajuan.com. Pinprompt is maintained by Juan and contributors.
